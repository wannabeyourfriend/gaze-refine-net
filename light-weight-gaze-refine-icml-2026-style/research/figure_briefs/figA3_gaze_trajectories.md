# Figure A3 — Gaze trajectories (kept from old appendix5)

## Why this figure
Visualizes raw gaze samples during the pre-fixation interval to
contextualize fixation noise. Reviewers like seeing raw data.

## Layout
1 × 3 row of trajectories: 25th, 50th, 75th percentile of waiting time.

## Subplots
For each percentile (t=0.67 s, 0.93 s, 1.33 s):
- Plot the 2-second raw eye-tracker gaze stream as a blue polyline with
  small dots at each sample.
- Overlay a red 3σ region around the eventual stable fixation mean.
- Mark the raw predicted (mean) point as a red plus.
- Title format: "25th percentile (t=0.67 s)" etc.

## Color & style
Same as old figure. Keep the blue trajectory + red region motif.

## Caption text
> Raw eye-tracker gaze trajectories during the pre-fixation interval for
> three representative trials at the 25th, 50th, and 75th percentile of
> waiting time. The red circle marks the 3σ region around the eventual
> stable fixation mean (red plus). After roughly 1 s, gaze stabilizes
> within the target region for the majority of trials.
